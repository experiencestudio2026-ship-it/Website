TEXT:
Place de la République is a large urban square designed as an important traffic junction in Paris. Its central monument represents Marianne, the personification of the French Republic, surrounded by allegories of Liberty, Equality and Fraternity. Although initially conceived within a system of urban control and circulation, the square has evolved into a major public space for political expression and collective gatherings.

On Saturday 17 October 2026, Place de la République became a major gathering point for demonstrations against the rising cost of living and fuel prices. The mobilization brought together students and other social movements. The event illustrates how the square continues to function as a central space for political expression, collective protest and public debate in Paris.
