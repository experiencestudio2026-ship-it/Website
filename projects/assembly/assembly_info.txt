TEXT:
Urban Radicals is a collective of architects who are interested in the roots of things. Their projects stem from an exploration of everyday life, function and the informal. The project ‘An Alternative Parliament for London’ comprises four installations that explore democracy and public debate in the city of London. This project examines the possibility of shifting democratic debate from closed institutions to the streets, squares and shared spaces. Which spaces allow us to engage in debate and discussion – whether formal or informal – within the free, public space that is the city?

This installation is an open-air auditorium, serving as a space for public debates and performances. Modelled on venues for political debates, the aim here is to bring the process of democratic debate into the city and onto the streets. The structure therefore takes the form of a continuous horseshoe-shaped bench, situated beneath an inflatable canopy, which fosters a sense of community and encourages reflection.
