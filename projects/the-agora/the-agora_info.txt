TEXT:
An outdoor public space where commercial, political, and social activities intersect. A place for meeting, exchange, discussion, and the formation of civic opinion. With the emergence of democracy in Athens, the Agora became one of the earliest examples of a public space associated with democratic practice and collective debate. Every adult male Greek citizen had the right to express his views freely on the current affairs of the city-state, bringing together collective efforts to make decisions that served the common good.
