Democracy & Space under construction…

About

Democracy & Space collects student projects on the architecture of democratic institutions: the rooms where parliaments, councils and assemblies meet, and how their spatial organisation shapes debate, visibility and power.

Every image in the space is a project. Drag to look around, move images to rearrange the space, and open a project by clicking it.
